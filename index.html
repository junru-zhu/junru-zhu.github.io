Junru Zhu, Shiming Xie, Aime Lu Fan Chen, Xiaoqing Ding, Chunxin Tang, Ruoyu Qi, Yulang Fei

Stress-Testing Structure-Aware Calibration of Malware Graph Neural Networks under Type Shift

Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi

arXiv:2609.28517, 2026.

Tests whether community-based graph features improve post-hoc calibration when previously unseen malware types appear. It shows how structural covariates can become unreliable outside their calibration support and evaluates a support-aware fallback.

Partition-Matched Evaluation of Community Features under Distribution Shift in Android Malware Function-Call Graphs

Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi

arXiv:2609.25256, 2026.

Compares graph-community descriptors with size-matched random partitions across Android malware distribution shifts. Its main contribution is a controlled evaluation protocol for determining whether mesoscopic structure provides genuinely shift-stable signal.

Residual Community Prototypes Under-Reject Held-Out Malware Families in FCG-MFD

Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi

arXiv:2609.24980, 2026.

Evaluates residual community prototypes for recognizing and rejecting malware families that were absent during training. The results distinguish changes to known-family geometry from a stable unknown margin and motivate matched controls and operational thresholds.

Token-Budgeted Escalation for Financial Document QA: Cost Is Predictable, Benefit Is the Bottleneck

Junru Zhu, Yixin Yang, Xiaoqing Ding, Ruoyu Qi

Public manuscript and research artifacts, 2026.

Formulates selective retrieval escalation as allocation under a shared token budget for financial-document question answering. The study finds that additional-call cost is predictable, while query-specific benefit remains the main allocation bottleneck.
